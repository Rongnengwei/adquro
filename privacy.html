Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what this Adquro website does with information. It covers the public site and the inquiry form. It is not a description of a live advertising exchange, and it should be reviewed by counsel before Adquro processes end-user advertising data.

Information you give us

If you write to sales@adquro.com or submit the form, we receive the name, work email, company, role, and message you choose to send. Your email app, not Adquro’s servers, creates that message when you use the form on this static site.

How that information is used

  • To answer your inquiry.
  • To understand whether there is a fit for a commercial conversation.
  • To keep a record of business correspondence.

What this site does not do

This marketing site does not run ad auctions, does not read device advertising IDs, and does not build audience profiles of people who only browse the page. It does not sell contact details from the inquiry form.

Sharing

Adquro may share an inquiry with people inside the company who need it to respond, and with email or hosting providers who process it only to deliver the service. Adquro may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Retention

Business correspondence is kept only as long as needed to respond, to manage a relationship, or to meet a legal record-keeping duty.

Your requests

You can ask Adquro to access, correct, or delete the contact details you sent, subject to applicable law. Write to privacy@adquro.com.

Updates

If this policy changes in a material way, the date at the top of this page will change.
